About the project

About VoiceBox

VoiceBox is a web-based and mobile complaint and suggestion management system created as a TVL-ICT capstone project for Lourdes College. It provides students with a convenient platform for submitting complaints, suggestions, concerns, and feedback while allowing submissions to be reviewed and managed digitally.

VoiceBox was developed by Fritz Vohn Manticahon Dayday .
